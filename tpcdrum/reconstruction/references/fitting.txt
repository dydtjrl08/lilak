TPCDrum Kalman-family fitting / smoothing — implementation and primary sources
2026-10-27. Mathematical implementation, not a statement of detector performance.

Primary sources checked

1. ACTS collaboration, Tracking in a nutshell, Kalman formalism and track fitting.
   https://acts.readthedocs.io/en/v27.0.0/tracking.html
   https://acts-project.github.io/class_acts_1_1_gain_matrix_smoother.html
   Basis: prediction x-=f(x), P-=F P F^T+Q; measurement update; backwards
   smoothing shares later measurement information with earlier states. Material
   transport and measurement calibration are distinct inputs. ACTS itself is not
   called by this module. Its performance cannot be attributed to this code.

2. H. E. Rauch, F. Tung, C. T. Striebel, Maximum likelihood estimates of linear
   dynamic systems, AIAA Journal 3 (1965) 1445–1450, doi:10.2514/3.3166.
   https://doi.org/10.2514/3.3166
   RTS gain C_k=P_filtered,k F_k^T inv(P_predicted,k+1).
   x_s,k=x_f,k+C_k(x_s,k+1-x_pred,k+1).
   P_s,k=P_f,k+C_k(P_s,k+1-P_pred,k+1)C_k^T.
   For UKF the sigma-point cross covariance replaces P_filtered F^T. This is a
   fixed-interval Gaussian smoother, not an independent second track fit.

3. S. J. Julier and J. K. Uhlmann, Unscented filtering and nonlinear estimation,
   Proceedings of the IEEE 92 (2004) 401–422, doi:10.1109/JPROC.2003.823141.
   https://doi.org/10.1109/JPROC.2003.823141
   University-hosted paper:
   https://www.ene.unb.br/gaborges/disciplinas/pe/papers/julier2004.pdf
   Basis: deterministic sigma points transform a mean and covariance through a
   nonlinear function. This implementation uses 2n+1 points and configurable
   alpha/beta/kappa; the numerical defaults are 1/2/0. No physical calibration is
   inferred from choosing UKF parameters. Sigma energies outside the supplied
   stopping-table domain cause failure rather than silent clipping.

4. J. Rauch and T. Schlüter, GENFIT — a Generic Track-Fitting Toolkit,
   J. Phys. Conf. Ser. 608 (2015) 012042; arXiv:1410.3698.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/1410.3698
   https://github.com/GenFit/GenFit
   Primary project API documentation for DAF:
   https://software.belle2.org/release-08-03-03/doxygen/classgenfit_1_1DAF.html
   GENFIT's DAF iterates weighted Kalman fits through an annealing schedule and
   supports noise hits and competing measurement hypotheses. Our implementation
   is narrower: one fixed observed space point per v, outlier-versus-inlier
   reweighting, and RTS after each pass. It is DAF-inspired, not a GENFIT binding
   and not GENFIT's full left/right or multi-hit ambiguity solution.

5. Geant4 collaboration, Physics Reference Manual, Multiple Scattering.
   https://geant4.web.cern.ch/documentation/dev/prm_html/PhysicsReferenceManual/electromagnetic/elastic_scattering/msc.html
   The manual gives the projected Gaussian-core Highland scale
   theta0=13.6 MeV * |z|/(beta*p) * sqrt(s/X0) * [1+.038 ln(s/X0)].
   It states the usual thickness validity interval 1e-3 <= s/X0 <= 100 and
   explains that Geant4 uses more detailed MSC models. Low-energy alpha transport
   in thin gas need not satisfy the approximation. The code therefore rejects
   out-of-range thickness unless the approximation is explicitly allowed, and
   flags any allowed extrapolation. A measured/exported energy-dependent angular
   diffusion table is a separate option. This is not a Geant4 transport clone.

What this implementation actually estimates

Physical coordinates: observation=(u,v,w) millimetres; v increases along the
configured downstream branch. State=(u,w,tu,tw,T), tu=du/dv and tw=dw/dv.
No magnetic field and no deterministic curvature term are included.

Mean segment transport:
  u_next=u+tu*Delta_v; w_next=w+tw*Delta_v; slopes unchanged in mean.
  path length s=Delta_v*sqrt(1+tu^2+tw^2).
  dT/ds=-S(T), S in MeV/mm, integrated with RK4 inside the tabulated domain.
The EKF differentiates this nonlinear energy transport numerically with guarded
one-sided differences at an endpoint. UKF propagates sigma points. Substep size
is a numerical accuracy setting, not a material property.

There is one explicit homogeneous material model between observation planes.
Dead windows, Si transport and material interfaces are not silently inserted.
The inverse-energy module handles a separately specified material path. A T prior
supplied to this fitter must already refer to the FIRST OBSERVED v. A Si energy
or a production-vertex energy is not that prior without the required transport.

Process noise:
  choose none, tuned, table, or highland explicitly.
  tuned angular_variance_rad2_per_mm is a tuning input, not measured TPC data.
  table requires energy_mev and projected_angular_variance_rad2_per_mm, with
  units energy=MeV and angular_variance=rad2/mm; no out-of-table interpolation.
  highland requires mass_mev, charge_e and radiation_length_mm explicitly.
  For projected angular variance theta^2, the slope covariance is
    S_slope=theta^2*(1+tu^2+tw^2)*[[1+tu^2,tu*tw],[tu*tw,1+tw^2]].
  A uniform diffusion approximation gives position/slope blocks
    Q_pp=Delta_v^2*S_slope/3, Q_pt=Delta_v*S_slope/2, Q_tt=S_slope.
  Energy noise Q_TT=straggling_variance_mev2_per_mm*s. The coefficient must be
  supplied; zero is allowed only as an explicit deterministic-loss approximation.
  Position-energy noise correlations within a segment are not modelled.

Measurement covariance:
  N x 2 x 2 means covariance of (u,w), at fixed v.
  N x 3 x 3 means covariance of (u,v,w); projected effective covariance is
    R_eff=J R_uvw J^T, J=[[1,-tu,0],[0,-tw,1]].
  This is a first-order treatment of uncertain v, not a full latent-surface fit.
  Updates use the Joseph covariance expression. Predicted/filtered/smoothed
  states, covariances, innovation residuals and whitened innovation pulls are
  retained. Round-off negative eigenvalues are removed; substantially indefinite
  covariances cause an explicit failure. No detector covariance is invented.

Outlier treatment:
  The measurement's own information is removed from its smoothed 2D marginal
  before computing an external/cavity residual. Let B be that marginal covariance
  and Rw=R/w: B_cavity=inv(inv(B)-inv(Rw)); the analogous information-vector
  subtraction gives its external prediction. Use R+B_cavity to whiten it.
  DAF-inspired weight w=1/(1+exp((chi2_external-chi2_cut)/(2*temperature))).
  Next pass uses R/w; temperatures decrease to 1. Huber reweighting is an option.
  Weights are not independently calibrated probabilities. Fixed-point change and
  convergence are exported; a finite iteration budget can leave nonconvergence.
  Very flexible process noise may still absorb outliers or real hard scattering.

Initialisation / configuration

linear_rts is the 4D geometry baseline. Its returned arrays retain five columns
but T and its covariance row/column are NaN; energy_treatment labels this.
Its automatic geometry seed is Theil-Sen-like and data derived, with deliberately
diffuse numerical geometry prior. An explicit independent prior is supported.
Energy-aware EKF/UKF require an explicit 5x5 initial_covariance: an enormous slope
variance is not a harmless diffuse prior when sigma-point path lengths affect
energy loss. Its energy variance must agree with energy_sigma_mev squared.
An optional initial_state also has to agree with the supplied T prior.

Minimum energy-aware configuration (VALUES MUST COME FROM YOUR INPUTS):
  method: ekf_rts or ukf_rts
  coordinate_system: physical_uvw_mm
  process_noise_mode: table / highland / tuned / none, explicitly selected
  energy_reference: first_point
  energy_sigma_mev: explicit
  initial_covariance: explicit 5x5 matrix
  straggling_variance_mev2_per_mm: explicit
Stopping table dictionary:
  energy_mev: increasing array
  dedx_mev_per_mm: same-length nonnegative array
  material: material identifier
  units: {energy: MeV, length: mm, dedx: MeV/mm}
  provenance: exporter/source/conditions, retained in the fit result
This module consumes the dedx columns; the inverse-energy module additionally
needs the range_mm column from the same table exporter.
Geant4's total stopping-power query can include electronic+nuclear stopping,
whereas its CSDA-range query follows the energy-loss tables. Their numerical
derivative/inverse relation is not assumed here. Closure between the separate
forward-dedx and inverse-range transports must be checked on actual exported
tables and reported before treating the reconstructed energy as validated.

Interpretation limits

The returned 'initial' tangent is at the first observed plane, AFTER any earlier
unobserved scattering. RTS propagates later measurements' information backwards;
it does not undo an individual random kick, make initial MC truth observable, or
prevent late measurements from affecting the earlier estimate. Propagating to a
production vertex needs a vertex estimate and a specified material/noise path.
No 120-degree or equal-energy constraint is used in this fitter or its checks.

Forward innovation chi2 and posterior measurement residual chi2 are distinct
diagnostics. Nominal 2N-4 geometric degrees of freedom is not automatically a
calibrated goodness-of-fit distribution when process noise, fitted energy,
data-derived seeds or robust weights are present. No p-value is fabricated.

The tests cover independent least-squares equivalence, known stopping transport,
unscented moment closure, synthetic curved/scattered trajectories, cavity-based
outlier rejection, covariance/innovation closure on known synthetic noise, and
failures for missing calibration or table/model-domain violations. They establish
software/mathematical behaviour ONLY. User's new equal-energy DDE digi, measured
space-point errors, drift conversion, gas stopping/scattering/straggling inputs,
and held-out detector-data accuracy are still unverified as of this implementation.
